About Pongo

OOH-OOH! 🦧 Greetings from the canopy! I’m Pongo, the original 'Man of the Forest' hanging out right here in the lush jungles of Malaysia (mostly over in Sabah and Sarawak). I might look like a big, relaxed ball of orange fluff who just loves snacking on wild figs, but I need you to listen to something important. My family is Critically Endangered. PongoBot: An AI-Powered Conservation Companion for Orangutan Education

Vinothini Kasinathan · Isaac Lew Zhi Hao · Bryan Wan Zhing Hoi · Chai Zhe Hee · Sarimah Samsudin

School of Computing, Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU), Malaysia

Abstract

Public awareness of orangutan conservation in Malaysia remains limited, particularly among younger generations who often lack access to engaging and interactive environmental learning resources. To address this issue, this project introduces PongoBot, an AI-powered educational chatbot designed to promote knowledge and awareness of orangutans and their conservation through conversational learning. PongoBot supports both English and Bahasa Malaysia and integrates interactive features such as quizzes, multimedia content, gamified activities, and personalised responses to enhance user engagement. Users can explore conservation topics through natural conversations while receiving immediate and accurate feedback. The project aims to provide an accessible and scalable digital learning tool that encourages environmental awareness and supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, particularly SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 13 (Climate Action), and SDG 15 (Life on Land). User evaluation was conducted using the Chatbot Usability Questionnaire (CUQ), involving 30 participants. The results indicated excellent usability, with a mean CUQ score of 95.6, demonstrating high levels of user satisfaction, engagement, and ease of use. The findings suggest that AI-driven chatbots can serve as effective tools for environmental education and conservation outreach. PongoBot demonstrates how artificial intelligence can be leveraged to create meaningful learning experiences while promoting biodiversity conservation and sustainable development.
